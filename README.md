# About me

I am a theoretical and computational physicist working in Computational Soft Matter Physics, currently pursuing a PhD** at the School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology (CCEB), Nanyang Technological University (NTU).

I enjoy solving physical problems through code, with an emphasis on clarity, maintainability, and performance. Most of what I build is for research or personal use, but I try to keep it practical and well-structured.

I am also a Linux and FOSS enthusiast who likes understanding systems deeply and improving them where possible.

## Selected projects

I regularly build small tools to support my workflow, writing, and research. A few of them:

- [leovim](https://github.com/leogabac/leovim): My `neovim` configuration for Python, C/C++, LaTeX, and whatever else I am using at the time.
- [novellum](https://github.com/leogabac/novellum): A CLI note-taking tool for LaTeX-heavy workflows.
- [gremux](https://github.com/leogabac/gremux): A `tmux` session manager.
- [suisave](https://github.com/leogabac/suisave): A simple backup tool configured through plain text files.
- [sysentropy](https://github.com/leogabac/sysentropy): An opinionated extension of Python's logging module.
