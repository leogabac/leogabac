# About me

I am a theoretical and computational physicist working in computational soft matter physics. I am currently pursuing a PhD in Chemical and Biomedical Engineering at NTU.

I enjoy solving physical problems through code, with an emphasis on clarity, maintainability, and performance. Most of what I build is for research or personal use, but I try to keep it practical and well-structured.

I am also a Linux and FOSS enthusiast who likes understanding systems deeply and improving them where possible.

## Current focus

- Machine learning and AI methods for physics, especially generative modelling and sampling
- Variational and flow-based generative modelling for glassy many-body systems
- Thermodynamics-aware machine learning for soft matter
